- Location: Ghana (Fieldwork 1 week)

Project detail copy aligned to the Ghana Figma mockup:
- Research Gap: local knowledge integration in climate services is growing, but practical implementation and social-cultural impacts remain underexplored.
- Objective & Use Case: analyze how local knowledge and community dynamics are represented and transformed through the DROP app in Ghana.
- Framework & Methodology: service design, systems thinking, soft impacts of innovation, and participatory fieldwork.
- Findings: local knowledge is contextual, embodied, gendered, and intergenerational; the app simplifies or shifts some of those meanings in practice.
- Discussion & Contribution: reflect on how climate services can bridge technical and local knowledge systems and support more responsible service design.
