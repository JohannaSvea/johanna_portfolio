Projects

Here you can explore a selection of my research and design projects that I have been working on recently. Situated in various interdisciplinary and international contexts, they reflect my interest in addressing complex social, cultural and environmental challenges and creating entry points for change.

Together, these projects showcase my experience in moving between abstract and micro-level analysis, between research and implementation, and between participatory processes of knowledge co-production and the development of informed outcomes.

Initiatives

Here you can explore the sustainability-focused initiatives that I am working on in my free-time. Most of them are still ongoing and some of them I have founded myself.

Across these projects, I have secured funding, built partnerships, coordinated teams, facilitated workshops, hosted events, conducted research and experiments, documented processes, but most importantly spent time and learned with like-minded people that share the same values and aspirations for a regenerative future.
